# Phase 07 — App icon and website look

**Goal (the author, 2026-11-16):** an app icon from Codex, without a white frame; graphics on the
website from Codex, and the site restyled after its proposal.

- **Icon:** Codex draws the silver khomus on midnight leather inside Apple's classic macOS tile
  (824 × 824 of 1024, continuous corners, soft shadow) — the shape macOS 26 accepts as an icon
  instead of putting it on a light plate. The alpha mask is copied from Didgeridoo's icon, which
  passed the same check in its phase 06.
- **Website:** Codex works on a copy of `docs/`, makes a hero illustration and an ornament band,
  and restyles `site.css` (and lightly `manual.css`) in the app's palette with serif headings.
  English and Czech pages change tag for tag; links, download URLs and versions stay.
- The prompt and Codex's design notes: `.planning/design/site/`.

Brumle

A playable synthesized jaw harp: twang, moving overtones, breath and rhythm, as an Audio Unit and a standalone app.

Download for macOS Version 0.1.0 for macOS 26 or later. Read the manual.

What it does

A jaw harp to play

The twang of the reed and the overtones that move as the mouth changes shape, with breath and rhythm behind them.

App and Audio Unit

A standalone Mac app and an Audio Unit instrument for Ableton Live, Logic Pro and other hosts, sharing one sound engine.

Played from MIDI

Play it from a keyboard or a controller, in the app or in your host.

Download

The disk image is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. Drag Brumle to Applications.
